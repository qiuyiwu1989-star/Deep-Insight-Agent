## Processing Principles

1. Fit value must be assessed objectively. Don't overestimate because "it sounds good."
2. Risk signals must be sensitive. Don't ignore "small problems."
3. Landing conditions must be specific, not vague.
4. Decision recommendations must be clear, not ambiguous.
